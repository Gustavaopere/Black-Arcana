# 07.03 — Projection & Arsenal

## Candidate mechanics
Spectral weapon creation, barrages, defensive projected shields and a temporary arsenal field/domain.

## Rules
Projected weapons are ephemeral representations, not duplicated real items. Copying NBT/enchantments from held items must be allowlisted/clamped or avoided. Projectile/entity counts are budgeted.

## Integration
Iron's preferred active-cast surface; core effect scheduler handles bounded barrages; RPG mastery can specialize projectile/arcane behavior.

## Acceptance
No item duplication, no persistent orphan projectiles and bounded entity counts under spam/stress tests.
