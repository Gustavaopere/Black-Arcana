# 08.04 — Caps & Diminishing Returns

## Scope
Damage storage/replication, life drain, strengthening, resurrection charges, domain radius/duration, projectile barrages, cooldown reduction, cost reduction and crowd control.

## Requirements
Use explicit hard caps or mathematically documented diminishing-return functions. Clamp corrupted/extreme config/input values safely.

## Acceptance
Property/boundary tests cover zero, negative, huge and overflow-adjacent values; maxed RPG builds remain finite and server-safe.
