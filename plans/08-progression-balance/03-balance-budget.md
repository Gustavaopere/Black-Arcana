# 08.03 — Quantitative Balance Budget

## Objective
Give spells comparable power accounting instead of tuning by feel alone.

## Dimensions
Single-target DPS/burst, AoE count/radius, control duration, mobility distance, survivability, utility, resource efficiency, cast time, cooldown, setup/ritual cost and risk.

## Work
Define tier envelopes and benchmark encounters/mobs from the actual pack. Compare Black Arcana output with Iron's/Ars and RPG scaling at representative progression points.

## Acceptance
Every production spell has a tier and documented budget rationale; outliers require explicit decision.
