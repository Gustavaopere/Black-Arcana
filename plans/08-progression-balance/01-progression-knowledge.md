# 08.01 — Knowledge & Unlock Progression

## Model
Unlocks may come from discoveries, rituals, mod progression milestones and mastery—not repetitive casting alone.

## Requirements
- Separate knowing a spell from being powerful enough to cast it.
- Persist unlocks with migration-safe ids.
- Optional mod milestones are queried through adapters.
- Admin/debug grant/revoke paths for testing.

## Acceptance
Unlock save/load and migration tests; missing integration cannot corrupt player progression.
