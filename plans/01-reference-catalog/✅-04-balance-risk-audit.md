# 01.04 — Balance & Risk Audit

## Objective
Identify dangerous mechanics before they become architecture.

## Review dimensions
- unbounded damage/resource growth;
- resurrection/invulnerability loops;
- item duplication or permanent strengthening exploits;
- block/fire/explosion griefing;
- chunk loading and entity spam;
- boss trivialization;
- PvP abuse/control denial;
- cross-mod resource conversion loops;
- automation exploits.

## Acceptance
Every high-risk mechanic has explicit caps, cooldown/resource budgets, server config and test cases—or is removed from scope.
