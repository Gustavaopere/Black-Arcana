# Visual 07A.06 — Sigils & Ritual Presentation

## Objective

Create an original Black Arcana sigil/ritual presentation language reusable by Black Arcana-owned surfaces without making visuals authoritative.

## Clean-room/provenance

Do not copy Mahou Tsukai projector images, circle textures, models, animations, sounds, text or other protected assets. Use original Black Arcana geometry/assets or separately compatible licensed material with provenance recorded. Reusable asset-production tooling follows `minecraft-mod-factory`.

## Bounded presentation definition

A sigil definition may describe presentation-only data such as stable presentation id, ring/arc/line primitives, original glyph references, palette token, bounded animation phase/speed, radius/height/opacity limits, telegraph role and an optional provider presentation key.

It contains no gameplay effect, script, command or arbitrary remote content.

## Visual grammar

- concentric rings: containment/ritual scope;
- broken rings: unstable/forbidden state;
- radial anchors: material/offering positions;
- directed lines: transfer/sourcing direction;
- central seal: ritual/spell identity;
- outer warning marks: hazard/world-policy state;
- Luminal/Umbral/Arcane palettes are presentation only.

This is a grammar, not authorization to create one bespoke copied texture per spell.

## Provider coordination

- Eidolon: coordinate with an altar/ritual surface only through a verified presentation seam; do not replace provider authority.
- Iron's: use Black Arcana sigils for Black Arcana-owned telegraph/channel readability only where they do not conflict with provider presentation.
- Ars Nouveau: do not replace Ars glyph rendering; add Black Arcana-owned ritual/fusion telegraphs only through a safe seam.

## Accessibility

Polarity and danger cannot be conveyed by color alone. Telegraphs need shape/readability differences. Reduced motion must retain timing/readability, and low-particle mode must retain target/hazard boundaries.

## Asset/network policy

Arbitrary remote URL imagery is out of scope. Future user/server-pack art requires a separate review with strict source, size, type, privacy/cache and provenance controls.

## Visual QA

Validate readability of ritual scope, anchors, transfer direction, central identity and warning state across accessibility presets and representative scenes, while confirming visual simplification never changes server timing or settlement.
