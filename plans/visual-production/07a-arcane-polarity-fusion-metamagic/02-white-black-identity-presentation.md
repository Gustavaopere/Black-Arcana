## Goal

Make Luminal and Umbral magic visually and informationally distinct without allowing color/style to determine server polarity.

## Umbral Codex presentation

The Codex may present server-authored discovered techniques, bargains/contracts, danger/corruption warnings, lore/provenance entries, progression requirements and whether power is stolen/consensual/self-owned.

Its final physical-item/UI/knowledge-surface form is not frozen. Presentation must never become spell execution, unlock mutation, resource authority or a second corruption/strain system.

## Ankh of Continuance presentation

The Ankh is a focus/token/presentation surface over canonical Soul Anchor/Mortal Ledger authority. Visual ritual feedback may communicate anchoring, consent and bounded charges but cannot create or settle a resurrection charge.

Eidolon presentation is optional and remains contingent on the runtime's exact caster/session identity gate.

## Luminal direction

- precise geometric symmetry;
- restrained gold/ivory/blue-white palette where original assets support it;
- protective/continuity motifs;
- clear telegraphs for consent/benefit and death-state anchoring.

## Umbral direction

- asymmetric, contractual and binding motifs;
- dark red/violet/black-gold palette where original assets support it;
- visible extraction link from source to beneficiary where gameplay readability benefits;
- explicit consequence feedback for Corruption/Strain.

These are presentation directions only. The server derives polarity from source and agency; a dark-looking effect is not automatically Umbral and a bright-looking effect is not automatically Luminal.
