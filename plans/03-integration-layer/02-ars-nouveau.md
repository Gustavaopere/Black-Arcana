# 03.02 — Ars Nouveau

## Intent
Integrate Ars where its source/mana, glyph or utility ecosystem adds value; do not recreate Ars spell crafting inside Black Arcana.

## Investigate
Official 1.21.1 addon APIs/events, resource access, spell context interoperability, source mechanics and safe ways to expose Black Arcana unlocks/components.

## Candidate uses
Displacement/utility variants, ritual ingredients, knowledge gates or glyph-adjacent effects where this avoids redundancy.

## Acceptance
Integration has no hard classloading dependency when Ars is absent and one representative resource/utility bridge passes server tests.
