# 03.04 — Malum

## Intent
Use Malum's spirit/soul economy and thematic content for soul/death magic without duplicating its systems.

## Investigate
Public 1.21.1 APIs/registries for spirits, soul data, items, rites/progression and damage/event hooks.

## Requirements
Black Arcana costs must consume real Malum resources atomically where selected. No infinite conversion loop between Black Arcana costs and Malum-generated rewards.

## Acceptance
One synthetic soul cost/effect uses supported Malum contracts and passes anti-duplication/resource accounting tests.
