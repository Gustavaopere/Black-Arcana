# 06.04 — Black Arcana Grand Rituals

## Scope
Only mechanics that cannot be cleanly hosted by existing ritual APIs and justify a bespoke world interaction.

## Candidate conditions
Sigil layout, moon/time, dimension/biome, catalyst blocks, soul/blood/item costs, knowledge prerequisites and multiple ritual phases.

## Constraints
No arbitrary structure scan every tick. Cache/index only active ritual anchors. World changes route through Stage 04 policy.

## Acceptance
One representative grand ritual survives chunk lifecycle as designed, has bounded validation cost and cannot double-consume/double-reward.
