# Casos de teste candidatos — Hemática/Vincular

- spell never consumes normal mana;
- sufficient blood + zero mana can pass resource gate;
- full mana + no blood fails;
- `NO_BLOOD` target cannot fund spell;
- linked source ownership/consent enforced;
- one source reservation cannot fund two casts;
- rollback exact;
- multiple links deterministic;
- source removal invalidates availability;
- blood reservoir restart/reload accounting exact;
- no conversion from vital energy/soul/spirit/mana by fallback.
