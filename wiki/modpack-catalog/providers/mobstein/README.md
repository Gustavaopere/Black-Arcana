## Publicly documented resurrection loop

The official guide describes the ordinary resurrection path through the **Clinical Stretch**:

1. place/use the Clinical Stretch at night;
2. place a compatible full body;
3. wait roughly 15 seconds;
4. lightning occurs as part of the resurrection presentation;
5. the corresponding resurrected creature becomes available.

The publisher also documents a **Lightningbolt Syringe** as a way to perform resurrection during the day.

These are provider-native acquisition/activation gates. Black Arcana must not replace them with a generic no-cost resurrection shortcut.

## Corporeal versus soul resurrection

Mobstein's public identity is materially physical:

- remains/body parts are collected;
- bodies are placed on machines;
- anatomical organs and syringes participate in reconstruction;
- reconstructed creatures become tameable companions/bodyguards or utility entities.

Therefore the semantic overlap with Black Arcana Souls & Death is real at the high-level word **resurrection**, but the resource/causal model is different. Black Arcana must preserve that distinction rather than silently turning Mobstein corpses into Black Arcana souls or vice versa.
