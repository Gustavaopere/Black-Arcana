## Current public identity

The current public project page describes Dreamless primarily as an **anti-mage / non-magical counterplay** expansion rather than a conventional large magic school. It adds curios, armor, weapons and related content for players who want to fight magic users without leaning deeply into normal spellcasting.

The provider also introduces the **Empty** school. Public documentation describes it as short-duration debuff spell counterplay that can only be cast while the caster has the required effect supplied by the school's armor setup.

The same project also adds weapon-oriented benefits for magic users in exchange for reduced mage-related attributes.

## Phase 2 classification correction

Dreamless is kept in `SPELL PROVIDER` because it explicitly exposes an Empty school with castable debuff capabilities. However, much of its content belongs to anti-mage gear rather than discrete spells.

Phase 2 therefore must not count every Dreamless item/curio as a spell. The future granular page will separate:

- Empty-school castable debuffs;
- anti-mage armor/set mechanics;
- melee/bow support bonuses;
- curios/weapons/resources.

## Deduplication impact

### Order / anti-magic

Dreamless proves that the pack already contains a dedicated anti-mage identity with castable debuff counterplay. Combined with Iron's `Counterspell`, Discerning's `Silence`, Ars `Dispel` and other providers, Black Arcana Order cannot justify itself as merely “magic suppression”.

A genuine Order delta must operate at a different authority layer: bounded seals/laws/constraints that change what actions are admitted in an area or contract, while remaining server-authoritative and compatible with provider-native denial.

### Empty / void / negation

Empty-school presentation should not be conflated with Black Arcana Chaos. Negation and anti-mage suppression are a separate semantic family from probability/entropy/reality instability.
