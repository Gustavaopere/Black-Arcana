## Disabled-by-default compatibility glyphs

Official current documentation lists `AOE II`, `AOE III`, `Amplifier II` and `Amplifier III` as copied compatibility content disabled by default. They are not counted as unique Ars Zero capability gaps for Black Arcana.

## Geometry augments

Historical current-line documentation previously exposed geometry-oriented augments such as Cube/Sphere/Flatten/Hollow. The current 2.0.2 public page confirms Geometrize and the exact 2.0.2 release confirms geometry-process security, but this audit does **not** promote historical augment registry IDs or active-state assumptions as exact 2.0.2 facts without the binary/source.

## Resource/cost boundary

Public docs prove Ars mana/Source participation, including Beam per-hit drain and Convergence Source interactions. They do not provide a complete exact 2.0.2 transaction/API contract for Black Arcana integration. Therefore:

- resource authority remains Ars/Ars Zero;
- no second mana resource is introduced;
- no guessed cost is recorded;
- any future observer/bridge must hook a provider-final causal event or transaction rather than replaying glyph execution.

## Registry completeness statement

`12 current unique glyph capabilities` means twelve distinct display-name capabilities evidenced by current official project/release material. It does **not** mean twelve exact registry objects were extracted from the installed JAR. Binary registry extraction remains pending and is a separate validation surface.
