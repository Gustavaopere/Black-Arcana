## Kinetic Pylon

The publisher documents conversion of nearby Apothic Enchanting stats into factory effects:

- **Eterna** → lower network stress use;
- **Arcana** → greater generated stress capacity;
- **Quanta** → temporary nearby player-gem stat boost.

The strongest overlapping Pylon applies according to the publisher description.

These are provider systems, not independent spell identities.

## Factory tools

- **Factory Locator** — traces moving items.
- **Factory Scanner** — groups nearby kinetic networks and reports RPM, stress, machine state and likely problems.
- **Apokinetic Wrench** — socket/tool interaction and, in 1.0.6, normal Create Wrench compatibility.
- **Apokinetic Table** — Rotation progression for gems.

These are not counted as spells.

## Deduplication consequences

Future Black Arcana design should not treat the following as unexplored magical gaps without first accounting for Apokinetics:

- machine speed enhancement;
- machine stress reduction;
- input preservation;
- output multiplication;
- machine range extension;
- thermal/fuel efficiency;
- cold-process enablement;
- fluid-machine enhancement;
- logistics acceleration;
- kinetic high-speed processing;
- machine breaking/contact damage.

This does not prohibit Black Arcana spells with superficially similar outcomes; it requires a real semantic distinction in authority, target, cost and gameplay role.
