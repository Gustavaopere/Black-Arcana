## Catalog rule

Nature's Ritual is treated as a provider-native preparation/ritual system. The master catalog closes recipe identity, inputs, outputs, salting, crop admission, duration and completion lifecycle.

Individual capability pages remain appropriate where an output itself introduces substantial behavior — for example Aegiflora, Astrylis, Grimshade, Lourdes, Morphora, Nautilite, Windsong, elemental nodes, Rootshaper, Kelpweave Blade, Sage Pendant and Bloomwrap armor. Those output-behavior audits must not be confused with the already-complete 19/19 recipe inventory.

Any future integration preserves Hexalia authority; a ritual tick or crop scan is not a Black Arcana cast/mastery event.
