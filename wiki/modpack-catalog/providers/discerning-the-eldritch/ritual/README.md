## Common acquisition/cast-source rules

`AbstractRitualSpell` always disables crafting. Complex rituals disable generic looting. Super-complex rituals reject SPELLBOOK and SWORD sources; complex non-super rituals reject SPELLBOOK. The base also makes ritual casts non-interruptible.
