- **Mage Light** — server-validated placement of a magical torch with provider-configured range.
- **Phalanx Charge** — uninterruptible spear/shield guarding channel followed by a thrust.
- **Mana Charge** — direct recovery of canonical Iron's mana during channeling.

- **Sense Evil** — bounded snapshot detection/highlighting of undead/configured entities and loaded spawners.
- **Illuminate Stellar** — latent staff attack spawning 3–4 target-seeking stars.
- **Unite Luna** — latent staff attack spawning a high-damage crescent/moon projectile.
- **Mystic Shield** — frontal barrier that stores absorbed attack information and releases a counterattack.
- **Divine Possession** — full-armor-set gated temporary all-school power equalization/possession effect.
- **Mana Mending** — continuous repair of a locked held damaged item.
- **Wizardlamp** — server-validated floating/waterloggable magical lantern placement.

Holy therefore already occupies lighting, guard/charge, mana recovery, projectile defense, detection, item-bound attacks, reflective shielding, school equalization and item repair. Black Arcana Order/Holy-adjacent concepts must remain materially distinct.
